# MarkView Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

MarkView does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No analytics or telemetry of any kind
- No user accounts
- No data leaves your Mac, with one exception: if a Markdown document you
  open references an image by URL, the app fetches that image so it can be
  displayed. No other network requests are made.
- Documents you open are read locally and never uploaded anywhere

Contact: https://github.com/seeeeeeong/markview/issues
